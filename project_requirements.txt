Project Title:
AI-Powered Student Stress Prediction and Wellness Recommendation System

Problem Statement:
Students experience stress due to academic workload, examinations, sleep patterns, and lifestyle factors. Identifying stress levels early can help improve mental well-being and academic performance.

Objective:
To predict student stress levels using machine learning and provide personalized wellness recommendations.

Expected Users:
- College Students
- School Students
- Academic Counselors

Expected Output:
- Stress Level Prediction (Low, Medium, High)
- Personalized Wellness Recommendations
